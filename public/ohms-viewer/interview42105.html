Segment Synopsis: Morrow recounts arriving in Vietnam in 1967 with little money and experience while having only a handful of personal contacts. Morrow then describes the process of obtaining press credentials: he leverages a stringer letter from UPI's Kate Webb alongside a letter from the Dartmouth Daily News to meet the South Vietnamese Ministry of Information's two-letter requirement, ultimately receiving accreditation from both the Saigon government and the U.S. military. Despite being officially credentialed, Morrow emphasizes that he had no journalism training and remained deeply uncertain about what he was doing.
