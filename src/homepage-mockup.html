The Project

Fifty years after the fall of Saigon, a bilingual archive that asks what the sources we trust can still tell us about the Vietnam War.

The Dartmouth Digital History Initiative and the Vietnam Studies Center at Fulbright University Vietnam are recovering, restoring, and re-interpreting a set of oral history interviews recorded more than two decades ago with Pham Xuan An — alongside new interviews, in Vietnamese, with the colleagues, family, and historians who knew him.

Together they invite audiences in the United States, Vietnam, and beyond to reconsider the stories we tell about the war, and the double life of the journalist at its center.
